Refund Policy

Please review this Refund Policy carefully before purchasing an InvoicePro subscription or any other paid service.

Contents

InvoicePro provides subscription-based and other paid services that allow users to manage invoices, estimates, clients, expenses, products, reports, and other business activities.

Because payments for InvoicePro services are processed through third-party payment providers, including Paystack where applicable, payments successfully completed for InvoicePro services are generally final.

By purchasing an InvoicePro subscription or other paid service, you acknowledge and agree to the terms of this Refund Policy.

01

Payments Are Final

No General Refunds

Payments successfully made for InvoicePro subscriptions or other paid services are non-refundable unless InvoicePro is legally required to provide a refund.

Once a payment has been successfully completed, InvoicePro does not provide refunds simply because a user changes their mind, stops using the service, purchases the wrong plan, or decides that the service is no longer required.

Users are responsible for reviewing the selected plan, pricing, billing period, and other purchase information before completing a payment.

02

Third-Party Payment Processing

InvoicePro uses third-party payment providers to process payments. Paystack may be used to process payments for InvoicePro services.

Payment processing may involve the collection and processing of payment information by the applicable payment provider. InvoicePro does not control the independent payment-processing systems, policies, or procedures of those providers.

The payment provider may have its own terms, privacy policy, security procedures, and payment-processing rules.

Where a payment has been successfully processed by the payment provider and the InvoicePro service has been made available, InvoicePro will generally not reverse or refund that payment.

03

Subscriptions and Recurring Payments

InvoicePro may offer paid subscription plans that operate according to the billing period selected by the user.

Where recurring billing applies, the subscription may renew automatically according to the applicable subscription terms until the user cancels it.

Cancelling a subscription does not automatically create a right to a refund for a payment that has already been successfully processed.

Users should cancel their subscriptions before the next billing date if they do not wish to continue with a recurring subscription.

04

Subscription Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription in accordance with the cancellation options provided by InvoicePro.

Cancellation generally prevents future subscription renewals. It does not automatically reverse payments that have already been completed.

After cancellation, access to paid features may continue until the end of the applicable paid subscription period, or may be limited or removed according to the applicable subscription terms.

05

Duplicate Payments

If you believe you were charged more than once for the same InvoicePro subscription or transaction, contact InvoicePro Support as soon as possible.

We may review the transaction information to determine whether a genuine duplicate payment occurred.

A duplicate payment is different from a standard refund request. If a duplicate charge is confirmed, InvoicePro may work with the applicable payment provider to determine the appropriate resolution.

06

Unauthorized Payments

If you believe a payment was made from your account without your authorization, contact InvoicePro Support immediately and provide the relevant transaction information.

We may investigate the account and transaction to determine what occurred.

You should also contact your bank or payment provider where appropriate, particularly if you believe your payment credentials or financial account have been compromised.

Reporting an unauthorized transaction does not guarantee a refund from InvoicePro.

07

Failed or Reversed Payments

A payment that fails, is declined, or is automatically reversed by a payment provider may not result in a completed InvoicePro subscription payment.

If your account shows an incorrect payment status after a failed, reversed, or interrupted transaction, contact InvoicePro Support with the relevant transaction details.

InvoicePro may verify the transaction status with the applicable payment provider before taking action.

08

Service Interruptions and Technical Issues

InvoicePro aims to provide reliable access to its services, but temporary interruptions, maintenance, technical failures, network problems, or third-party service interruptions may occasionally occur.

A temporary interruption or technical problem does not automatically create a right to a refund.

Where appropriate, InvoicePro may investigate significant service issues and determine whether any corrective action is necessary.

Nothing in this section limits any rights or remedies that cannot legally be excluded under applicable law.

09

Chargebacks and Payment Disputes

If you believe a payment was processed incorrectly, we encourage you to contact InvoicePro Support before initiating a payment dispute or chargeback.

We will review legitimate transaction concerns and provide relevant information where appropriate.

Submitting a chargeback does not automatically establish that a refund is owed.

InvoicePro reserves the right to provide transaction records, subscription information, account activity, and other relevant information to the applicable payment provider or financial institution when responding to a payment dispute.

10

Changes to This Refund Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time to reflect changes to InvoicePro, our pricing, payment processes, services, legal requirements, or business practices.

When changes are made, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

Changes to this policy will not normally affect payments that were already successfully completed before the change, except where required by applicable law.
