At InvoicePro, we use cookies and similar technologies to help provide a reliable, secure, and convenient experience when you use our website and platform.

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, the purposes for which InvoicePro may use them, and the choices available to you when managing cookies through your browser or device.

This policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

01

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites may place on your browser or device when you visit a website. They allow a website to recognize a browser or remember information about a previous visit.

Cookies can be temporary or persistent. Temporary cookies may be removed when you close your browser, while persistent cookies may remain on your device until they expire or are removed.

InvoicePro may also use similar technologies, such as local storage, session technologies, pixels, or other technical methods that perform functions similar to cookies.

02

How InvoicePro Uses Cookies

InvoicePro may use cookies and similar technologies for purposes including:

  • Keeping users signed in
  • Maintaining secure sessions
  • Remembering account and platform preferences
  • Supporting website and application functionality
  • Improving platform performance
  • Understanding how features are used
  • Detecting unusual or suspicious activity
  • Protecting accounts and the platform
  • Diagnosing technical problems
  • Improving the overall user experience

The specific cookies or technologies used may change as InvoicePro develops and improves its services.

03

Essential Cookies

Some cookies and similar technologies are necessary for InvoicePro to function properly. These technologies may be used to maintain secure sessions, authenticate users, remember required settings, and support core platform functionality.

Because these technologies may be necessary for the operation and security of the service, they may not be available for optional disablement through ordinary cookie preferences.

Essential for the Platform

Essential technologies help InvoicePro provide secure account access and core functionality.

04

Preference Cookies

InvoicePro may use cookies or similar technologies to remember preferences that make the platform easier to use.

If these technologies are disabled, certain preferences may need to be selected again during future visits.

05

Security Cookies and Technologies

InvoicePro may use cookies or similar technologies as part of security and fraud-prevention processes.

These technologies may help us identify suspicious activity, protect authentication sessions, prevent unauthorized access, and investigate security incidents.

Security-related technologies may operate automatically when you use the platform and may be necessary to protect InvoicePro and its users.

07

Some services used by InvoicePro may be provided by third parties. Those providers may use cookies, software development tools, APIs, or similar technologies to provide their services.

Examples may include providers involved in areas such as:

08

Managing Cookies Through Your Browser

Most modern web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings.

Depending on your browser, you may be able to:

  • View cookies stored on your device
  • Delete existing cookies
  • Block cookies from specific websites
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Allow cookies only for selected websites
  • Configure your browser to notify you before cookies are stored

The exact controls available depend on the browser and device you use.

Changing your browser settings may affect your experience with InvoicePro and other websites.

09

What Happens If Cookies Are Disabled?

You may be able to access portions of the InvoicePro website without accepting optional cookies. However, disabling or blocking certain cookies may affect the functionality of the platform.

Depending on which technologies are disabled, you may experience issues such as:

  • Being unable to remain signed in
  • Having preferences reset
  • Reduced functionality
  • Additional authentication requests
  • Features not operating as intended
  • Reduced performance or personalization

Essential security and authentication technologies may continue to operate where they are necessary to provide the service or protect the platform.

10

Cookie Retention

The length of time a cookie remains on your device depends on whether it is a session cookie or a persistent cookie and on the purpose for which it is used.

Session technologies are generally removed or expire when the relevant browser session ends, while persistent technologies may remain for a defined period or until removed by you.

Retention periods may vary depending on the technology, service, security requirement, and purpose involved.
