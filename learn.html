Getting Started

Create Invoices

Learn how to create, review, save, edit, duplicate, download and manage professional invoices.

1

Open Your Invoice Page

Sign in to InvoicePro and open Invoices from the dashboard sidebar. You can also start from the dashboard's Create Invoice action.

2

Start a New Invoice

Click Create Invoice. InvoicePro opens the invoice form and prepares the next invoice number using your business invoice numbering settings.

3

Enter Invoice Details

Enter the invoice title, project name, reference number, purchase order, invoice number and currency. These details identify the document and can be shown in the invoice preview.

4

Select the Client

Choose a saved client from the client list. The client's name, company, email, phone and address can then populate the invoice automatically. If the client does not exist yet, create the client first from the Clients page.

5

Set Dates and Payment Terms

Choose the issue date, due date and payment terms. The due date can be calculated from the selected payment terms, while the date controls prevent invalid date combinations.

6

Add Products or Services

In Invoice Items, add every product or service being billed. Enter the item name, quantity and unit price. InvoicePro calculates each line total and updates the invoice subtotal automatically.

7

Apply Tax, Discount and Shipping

Use the totals controls to apply a tax percentage, discount and shipping charge. InvoicePro recalculates the subtotal and grand total whenever these values change.

8

Set Payment Information

Choose the payment status and review the payment information used on the invoice, including payment account, bank, provider, account number, payment reference, due-day information and payment instructions when available.

9

Add Notes, Terms and Signature

Add notes and terms and conditions for the client. You can also enter a signature name and title and provide a signature image when required.

10

Review the Live Preview

Use the invoice preview to confirm the business information, logo, customer details, invoice number, dates, items, totals, payment information, notes, terms and signature. The preview updates as you edit the form.

11

Save as Draft or Save the Invoice

Choose Save Draft when the invoice is not ready to issue. Choose Save Invoice when you are ready to create the invoice record. Invoice limits are checked before an invoice is created.

12

Manage an Existing Invoice

From the invoice table you can search, filter by status, filter by date, sort records, open an invoice, edit it, update its status, duplicate it, delete it, download or print its PDF, export invoice data and send it to the client.

13

Export Invoice Records

Use the invoice export menu to export invoice information as PDF, Excel or CSV. Pagination and table filters help you work with large invoice lists.

14

Download or Print an Invoice

Open the invoice preview and choose Download PDF or the print option. The preview also supports zoom and fullscreen controls for reviewing the document before saving or printing it.

15

Create an Estimate

Open Estimates and click Create Estimate. Enter the estimate title, project name, reference number, purchase order, estimate number and currency, then select a client, issue date and expiry date.

16

Build the Estimate

Add products or services as estimate items with quantities and rates. Apply tax, discount and shipping, then add notes, terms and conditions, a validity message, payment method and signature.

17

Preview and Save the Estimate

Review the estimate preview and choose Save Draft or Save Estimate. Saved estimates can later be edited, previewed, exported, approved, sent or converted.

18

Convert an Estimate Into an Invoice

Open the estimate and choose Convert. Confirm the conversion when prompted. InvoicePro transfers the estimate information into an invoice so you do not have to enter the client, items, totals and related information again.

19

Download a Document as PDF

For invoices and estimates, open the document preview and use the PDF action. The document is prepared from the current preview information so you can keep a copy, print it or share it with your client.

User Guide

Manage Clients

Learn how to add, organize, view, edit, export and use your client records throughout InvoicePro.

1

Open Clients

Choose Clients from the dashboard sidebar. The page shows your client list together with client statistics and actions for working with each record.

2

Add a Client

Click Add Client and enter the client's name, company, email address and phone number. You can also upload an optional client image.

3

Add Business Information

Store the client's tax ID, status, address lines, city, state or ZIP information and country. This information can later appear in invoices and estimates.

4

Save and Identify Clients

Save the client record. If an image is available, InvoicePro can use it beside the client's name; otherwise the interface can fall back to the client's initials.

5

View Client Details

Open a client to see their stored information, total estimates, total invoices, outstanding balance, paid invoices, pending invoices, overdue invoices, total revenue and last invoice information. Their estimate and invoice records are also shown.

6

Edit or Delete a Client

Use the client actions to edit saved information or delete a client after confirming the deletion.

7

Send Documents to a Client

From client-related actions you can send an estimate or invoice. The send forms can load the selected document, client information, invoice items, payment information, notes, terms, signature and a custom message before sending.

8

Export Client Records

Use the export menu to export client information as CSV, Excel or PDF.

User Guide

Track Payments

Learn how InvoicePro records payment-related information, shows invoice status and helps you monitor money that has been paid or is still outstanding.

1

Understand Invoice Status

InvoicePro separates invoices by status such as Draft, Pending, Paid and Overdue. These statuses are used throughout the invoice list, dashboard and reports.

2

Review Invoice Statistics

The invoice page summarizes total invoices and counts for draft, paid, pending and overdue invoices. Growth information is displayed alongside the statistics when available.

3

Update an Invoice Status

Open an existing invoice and use its status controls when a payment or invoice state changes. The updated status is reflected in the invoice records and related summaries.

4

Review Paid Invoices

Open Paid Invoices to see invoices that have been marked as paid. The page provides a paid total, invoice count and the individual paid invoice records when available.

5

Review Upcoming Payments

Open Upcoming Payments to review payment records that are expected in the future. If the data cannot be loaded, the page provides a retry action.

6

Review Subscription History

Open Subscription History to review subscription transactions and open payment details. The payment detail view includes transaction information, payment timeline, receipt information and options to download or print the receipt.

7

Record Business Expenses

Open Expenses and click Add Expense. Enter the amount, expense date and category, then optionally add a vendor, description and receipt attachment before saving.

8

Filter and Manage Expenses

Use the expense filters to search by date range and vendor. You can open an expense, edit it, delete it and review its receipt when one has been attached.

9

Review Expense Reports

The Expenses page provides category and monthly spending information, while its report tools can generate expense summaries for a selected reporting period.

User Guide

Reports & Analytics

Learn how to review revenue, invoices, clients, taxes, estimates, payment methods and detailed business reports.

1

Open Reports

Open Reports from the application navigation. The reports page loads your available invoice and estimate information and prepares the reporting dashboard.

2

Choose a Reporting Date Range

Use the date-range control to view a predefined period or choose a custom start and end date. Apply the filter to refresh the report information for that period.

3

Review Revenue

Review total revenue and the revenue overview chart. Revenue can be viewed by reporting period to make changes in business performance easier to understand.

4

Review Invoice Statistics

Reports summarize draft, pending, paid and overdue invoices, including their counts, amounts and percentages. Outstanding invoice information is also included in the report summary.

5

Review Revenue by Client

Use the client revenue panel to see which clients contribute to your recorded invoice revenue and compare client-level totals.

6

Review the Tax Summary

The tax summary shows taxable revenue, total tax, average tax and the number of taxable invoices so you can review the tax information contained in your invoice records.

7

Review Estimate Statistics

Reports include total estimates, draft estimates, approved estimates and estimated value, giving you a summary of your estimate pipeline.

8

Review Payment Methods

Where payment information is available, the payment-method report groups recorded invoice payment information so you can understand how clients are expected to pay.

9

Read the Detailed Invoice Report

The invoice report table gives you a detailed view of invoices included in the selected reporting range. Use it to inspect the underlying records behind the summary figures.

10

Export Your Report

Use the report export controls to generate a CSV, Excel or PDF version of the available report data.

User Guide

Send & Get Paid

Learn how to prepare documents for clients, send invoices and estimates, and configure the payment information shown to customers.

1

Make Sure the Client Has an Email

Before sending an invoice or estimate, make sure the selected client has a valid email address saved in their client record. InvoicePro checks for client email information when preparing the send flow.

2

Send an Invoice

Open an invoice and choose Send Invoice. The send modal loads the invoice items and payment information and lets you review the document before sending it to the client.

3

Add a Client Message

Enter a message for the client before sending. You can review the invoice notes, terms, signature and payment details that will accompany the document.

4

Send an Estimate

From a client or estimate workflow, select an estimate and choose the send action. InvoicePro loads the estimate and client information, lets you choose the estimate to send and then sends it using the prepared client email.

5

Configure Payment Details

Open your Profile and configure the payment information you want to use, including payment method, provider, bank name, account name, account number, routing or sort code, SWIFT/BIC, payment link, payment account, payment terms, custom payment period and payment instructions.

6

Save Payment Information

Review the payment fields and click Save Changes. InvoicePro uses the saved information when payment details are available for invoice and estimate previews and client-facing documents.

7

Monitor the Result

After sending or updating payment-related information, return to the invoice, payment and dashboard areas to monitor the document status and payment-related summaries.

User Guide

Customize Everything

Learn how to personalize your business information, invoice branding, profile, account settings, subscription and security controls.

1

Update Your Business Profile

Open Business Profile to manage your business logo, name, email, phone, website, address, tax ID or VAT number, currency, default language, time zone and registration number.

2

Set Invoice and Estimate Prefixes

Use the business profile settings to control your invoice prefix and estimate prefix. These settings are used when InvoicePro prepares document numbers.

3

Work With Products and Services

InvoicePro documents support products and services as invoice and estimate line items. When creating a document, add each item by entering its name, quantity and rate, then review the calculated total before saving.

4

Open the Invoice Template Studio

Open Template Design Studio to customize the structure and appearance of your invoice template. Select sections such as Header, Company Information, Customer Information, Invoice Details, Items Table, Totals, Notes, Payment Information and Footer.

5

Customize Template Appearance

Template settings can control colors, typography, text alignment, text transform, font style, width, alignment, shadow, borders, visibility and other visual properties for the selected invoice section.

6

Use Template Presets

Choose a preset such as Modern, Minimal, Corporate or Creative when you want a ready-made visual direction instead of configuring every setting manually.

7

Save or Restore Template Changes

Save your template when the design is ready. You can also reset changes or restore the default template when you want to return to the original design.

8

Manage Your Personal Profile

Open Profile to view and update your personal information. You can edit your email, phone number and country, upload or change your profile photo and manage payment details.

9

Manage Account Settings

Open Settings to manage your account, business information, notification preferences, subscription, security, data export and privacy controls.

10

Change Your Password

Use the security controls in Settings to enter your current password, choose a new password and confirm it before saving the change.

11

Manage Notifications

Settings provides notification controls for events including invoice paid, invoice overdue, invoice viewed, estimate accepted, estimate rejected and subscription updates.

12

Manage Your Subscription

Open Subscription to view your current plan, limits and billing information, then choose another plan when an upgrade is needed. Available payment options include card and PayPal flows.

13

Understand Subscription Payment Methods

When selecting a paid plan, choose the available payment method, review the order summary and complete the payment flow. InvoicePro can verify the resulting payment and update the subscription state.

14

Manage Auto-Renewal and Subscription Settings

Use the subscription controls in Settings to review renewal information and manage automatic renewal when the option is available for your account.

15

Export Your Account Data

Settings includes a data export option for retrieving the account information made available by InvoicePro. Use this when you need a copy of your account data.

16

Delete Your Account

If you decide to leave InvoicePro, the Settings page provides a delete-account flow that asks for confirmation and the account password before the deletion is processed.

17

Manage Sessions and Logout

Open Logout Settings from the account management area to review session options and log out of the current account or log out of InvoicePro across devices when that action is required.
