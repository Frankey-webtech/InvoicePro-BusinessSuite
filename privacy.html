At InvoicePro, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you provide when using our platform.

This Privacy Policy explains what information InvoicePro collects, how we use and protect it, when it may be shared, and the choices you have regarding your information.

By creating an InvoicePro account or using our services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

01

Information We Collect

We collect information that is necessary to provide, maintain, secure, and improve InvoicePro.

1.1 Account Information

When you create or manage an InvoicePro account, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Account credentials and authentication information
  • Profile information
  • Profile picture or logo, where provided
  • Business name and business details
  • Country and currency preferences
  • Account and subscription information

1.2 Business and Financial Information

InvoicePro allows you to create and manage business records. Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • Client names and contact information
  • Customer addresses and business information
  • Invoice information
  • Estimate information
  • Product and service information
  • Expense records
  • Payment and transaction records
  • Tax and discount information
  • Invoice notes and payment terms
  • Business registration or other business information you choose to enter
  • Reports and financial summaries generated through the platform

You are responsible for ensuring that you have the appropriate permission to provide information about your clients, customers, employees, or other individuals to InvoicePro.

1.3 Payment and Subscription Information

If you purchase an InvoicePro subscription or other paid service, payment information may be processed through our payment providers.

InvoicePro may receive information such as payment status, transaction references, subscription status, and related billing information.

We do not need to store your complete payment card details in order to provide subscription services. Where payment processing is handled by a third-party payment provider, that provider's privacy policy and terms may also apply.

1.4 Information You Upload or Create

You may upload or create information while using InvoicePro, including:

  • Business logos
  • Profile images
  • Signatures
  • Invoice documents
  • Client records
  • Estimates
  • Expense information
  • Product and service records
  • Other files or information you choose to store on the platform

1.5 Technical and Usage Information

When you access InvoicePro, certain technical information may be collected automatically, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Pages or features accessed
  • Login and session information
  • Date and time of activity
  • Error and diagnostic information
  • General usage information

This information helps us maintain reliability, detect security problems, understand how the platform is used, and improve our services.

02

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for legitimate business and service-related purposes, including to:

  • Create and manage your InvoicePro account
  • Provide invoice, estimate, expense, client, and business-management features
  • Generate documents and financial reports
  • Process subscriptions and payments
  • Maintain your account and preferences
  • Authenticate users and protect accounts
  • Send important account and service notifications
  • Provide customer support
  • Respond to questions and requests
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorized activity
  • Monitor and improve platform performance
  • Fix technical problems and errors
  • Develop and improve InvoicePro features
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations
  • Protect the rights, property, and security of InvoicePro, our users, and others

We do not use your business information for purposes unrelated to providing and improving InvoicePro without an appropriate legal basis or your permission where required.

03

How We Handle Your Business and Client Data

InvoicePro is designed to help businesses manage their own records.

Information that you enter about your clients, customers, vendors, invoices, expenses, and other business activities remains associated with your account and is processed so that InvoicePro can provide the services you request.

You are responsible for the accuracy of the information you enter and for ensuring that your collection and use of third-party information complies with applicable privacy laws.

InvoicePro does not claim ownership of the business information you create or upload solely because you store it on the platform.

  • Payment processing
  • Email and notification delivery
  • Security and fraud prevention
  • Technical monitoring

  • Analytics and platform performance

We may disclose information when we reasonably believe disclosure is necessary to:

  • Enforce our Terms of Service or other agreements

Business Transfers

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or destruction.

Depending on the information and circumstances, these measures may include:

  • Secure authentication
  • Access controls
  • Encryption where appropriate

  • Monitoring for suspicious activity

  • Understand platform usage
  • Improve performance
  • Detect security issues
