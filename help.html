Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an invoice?

Open the Invoices section from your dashboard and select the option to create a new invoice. Add your client, items, pricing and other invoice details, then save the invoice.

Can I convert an estimate into an invoice?

Yes. InvoicePro allows you to convert an estimate into an invoice so you do not have to recreate the client's information and items manually.

Can I send invoices to my clients?

Yes. You can send invoices directly to your clients from InvoicePro using the available invoice sending options.

Can I download my invoices?

Yes. InvoicePro allows you to generate and download professional invoice documents for your records and for sharing with clients.

How do I track invoice payments?

InvoicePro lets you manage invoice statuses and track which invoices are paid, pending, overdue or still require attention.

Can I manage my business information?

Yes. Your business information, profile details, branding and other available account preferences can be managed from Settings.
